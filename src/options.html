PaperCard

Turn anything you read into a card worth sharing.

Quick start

  1. Social posts Right-click a post and choose “Copy post as image”. On X and Reddit, it's also in the Share menu as “Copy as image”. XRedditThreadsInstagramBluesky WeiboZhihuXiaohongshu
  2. Any web page Select text, right-click, then choose “Copy selection as image”.
  3. Keyboard Select text or hover a post, then press Alt+Shift+S

The image goes straight to your clipboard. Paste it anywhere with Ctrl/Cmd + V.
